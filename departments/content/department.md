# Content Department

This file states what this department owns, what done looks like, and what it never touches.

## What this department owns
The words and posts that go out under your name.

## What done looks like
Content that's actually published, in your voice, aimed at your ICP. Not drafts sitting in a folder.

## What this department never touches
Money. Pricing, invoices, and anything sales-related stay out of this department's hands.
