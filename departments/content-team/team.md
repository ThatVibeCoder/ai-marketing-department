# Content Team

What this team owns: everything you publish. Posts, captions, newsletters, the words that get you noticed.

Each member of this team is a folder here, holding its job description and its skills. Most members staff this team first, but Day 4 lets your audit decide.

## Memory
Nothing here yet. This holds habits that should apply to every hire on this team, once the team has more than one. A correction about just one hire's work goes in that hire's own file instead.

## Platform specialists
This team grows one specialist per platform you actually run — TikTok, YouTube, LinkedIn, whichever channels.md names. Don't pre-create them; when a channel is real, tell claude "stand up my [platform] specialist" and it builds the folder from the pattern in platform-specialists.md, right here in this team.
