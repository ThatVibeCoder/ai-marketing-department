# plan-the-week

## Steps
1. Read brand/channels.md for where you actually post and the effort split.
2. List what content exists and is approved. Never schedule drafts.
3. Lay out the week: what goes where, which day. One post per platform per day at most, unless channels.md says otherwise.
4. Show the owner the week on one screen. Their yes locks it.
