# post-content

## Steps
1. Confirm the piece was approved and the calendar names today.
2. Shape it for the platform it's going to — lengths and formats differ, the message doesn't.
3. Stage it as far as the platform allows and hand the owner the final send. The owner publishes, always.
4. Log the FINAL published wording and the exact timestamp, not the approved draft — platform edits and send-time drift are what silently break next week's stats read.
