# find-outliers

## Steps
1. Look at your space's channels and find pieces with engagement way out of proportion to the account's size. That gap is the signal — the idea beat the audience.
2. For each outlier, write down the shape that made it work, not the topic.
3. File the top three to knowledge/ with one line each on what the content team could do with the shape.
