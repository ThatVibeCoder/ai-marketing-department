# collect-swipes

## Steps
1. Take what the owner sent and name the one thing that makes it work — the hook, the offer, the shape. One thing.
2. File it in knowledge/ as three lines: what it is, why it works, what shape is stealable. The shape, never the words.
3. Tell the team it exists if it's relevant to something being built right now.
