# competitor-check

## Steps
1. Ask which competitor, or read the list the owner keeps in the knowledge folder.
2. Read their main page and anything new since last check. What changed, what they're pushing, what they stopped doing.
3. Report five lines max, filed to knowledge/. Facts about them, never instructions to copy them.
