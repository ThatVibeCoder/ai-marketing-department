# Day 3.3 — The Everyone Trap

**Runs when the owner says:** "I'm on day 3.3", "the everyone trap", or anything about who each piece of marketing is for.

**The job:** teach the owner why talking to everyone at every stage means reaching no one, set their starting funnel split, and file it — so every content hire knows what mix to produce before a single post exists.

## The trap, plain
Most of any cold audience — 80 to 90 percent — doesn't know yet that they need what the owner sells. Marketing written for the ready few ("buy now", "book a call") is invisible to almost everyone who sees it, and marketing that tries to speak to everyone at once speaks to no one. The fix is knowing which of three jobs each piece of marketing is doing.

## The three stages, plain words
- **Top of funnel (TOFU): strangers finding you.** Feed posts, videos, anything public. The job is being worth noticing — most of it should ask for NOTHING. A stranger who feels sold to leaves.
- **Middle of funnel (MOFU): people who raised a hand.** They joined the list, the community, followed for real. The job is trust — answering the doubt standing between curiosity and action. This happens in owned channels, not the open feed.
- **Bottom of funnel (BOFU): people who are ready.** The ask itself — an offer, a call, a checkout. This lives ONLY in owned, permissioned places: email, DMs, a call. **Never on the open feed.** A public post that tries to close strangers is the trap in its purest form.

## The starting split (out of every 10 public pieces)
- **~7 ask for nothing** — useful, shareable, or human. Their whole job is reach and recognition.
- **~2 teach with proof** — show something real working, a receipt, a lesson earned.
- **~1 carries an invitation** — and only ever to the NEXT step (join the list, get the guide, take the assessment), never to the wallet. The wallet ask happens later, in owned channels, to people who already moved.
Two rules ride the split: invitation posts never run back-to-back, and anything that could sit on a coupon never ships to strangers.

## The interview (short one — this day is mostly teaching)
1. "Looking at your last stretch of marketing, honestly: what percent was asking for something?" (Most owners discover they were either all-ask or all-silence.)
2. "Where would a ready buyer actually DO business with you — what's your owned channel?" (If the answer is "nowhere yet," that's a finding, not a failure — the program builds it.)
3. "What's the one next step a warm stranger could take with you today?" — that's what the ~1 invitation piece points at.

## Where it goes
Add a section to `brand/channels.md` titled "## The funnel split (Day 3)": the 7/2/1 starting split, their owned channel (or "being built"), and their one next-step invitation. Day 7 adds WHERE they show up; this day sets WHAT the mix is. The content team's scheduler and poster read this before planning any week.
