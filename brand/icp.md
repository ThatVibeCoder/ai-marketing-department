# ICP

Day 3 fills this in. Right now it's just the shape.

## Who
The actual person you sell to. Not a category, a real person you could picture.

## Current State
What's actually frustrating them right now, in their own words.

## Dream State
What they actually want to be true instead.

## What Makes Them Click
The kind of proof that gets their attention.

## What Does Not Work On Them
What turns them off completely.

## Who They're Not
Just as important as who they are.
